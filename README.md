# batteryfast.github.io

An independent compatibility guide for Dyson and Roomba vacuum replacement batteries. This site helps consumers match voltage, capacity, SV codes and part numbers before purchasing — no sales, just verified fit data.

## What This Project Does

Finding the right battery for your vacuum cleaner is harder than it should be. Most listings show stock photos, not your actual battery label. This guide bridges the gap:

- Dyson models: V6, V7, V8, V10, V11, V15 — including compatibility charts and genuine vs aftermarket comparisons
- iRobot Roomba models: 500 through 900 series, plus Braava and Combo variants
- Buyer guides: How to read battery labels, understand voltage and capacity, and check your rights under Australian Consumer Law and UK Consumer Rights Act

Visit the live site at [batteryfast.github.io](https://batteryfast.github.io/) to browse full compatibility tables and detailed model breakdowns.
