Battery warranty and returns in Australia, the UK and the US

Warranty language is easy to skim and easy to misunderstand. This guide separates voluntary seller promises from statutory consumer rights.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Australia - ACL and ACCC

The Australian Consumer Law gives consumers automatic guarantees that cannot be excluded by a business term. ACCC guidance explains that warranties are additional: a warranty cannot replace, change or remove those basic rights. A major problem generally gives the consumer a choice of refund or replacement, while a minor problem is usually fixed by free repair.

Australia - ACL and ACCCConsumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law apply automatically and cannot be excluded by a store warranty. A seller's written warranty is additional to those rights. Ask for warranty and return terms in writing before checkout.

When buying a replacement battery, ask the seller to state the warranty period in writing, what evidence is required and who pays return shipping. For a second source of product listings, see AussieBatt replacement vacuum batteries.

United Kingdom - Consumer Rights Act 2015

Goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. The short-term right to reject is generally 30 days; after that, a repair or replacement can be required, and further remedies apply if that fails. For a fault appearing in the first six months, the trader generally has to show it was not present at delivery.

United KingdomThe Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you remedies against the retailer for goods that are not of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose or as described. Check delivery, returns and recycling terms before you buy.

Check whether the seller is UK-based, who handles returns and what delivery terms apply. UK buyers can review the Batteryfast warranty and returns guide and UK vacuum cleaner batteries at drill-battery.shop for local delivery options.

United States

There is no single federal equivalent to the Australian or UK regime for this product category. Read the seller's written warranty, return window and restocking terms before checkout. Keep proof of purchase and photograph the battery label on arrival.

United StatesConfirm the exact part number and the seller's return window. For disposal, use a certified battery drop-off or household hazardous waste program; do not put lithium-ion packs in household bins.

For general background, see Batteries Company battery information.

A checklist before you pay

  1. Confirm the fit in writing.
  2. Read the full warranty text, not the headline.
  3. Check who pays return freight.
  4. Ask how defects are assessed.
  5. Keep the packaging until the pack is tested.

Frequently asked questions

Does using an aftermarket battery void my rights in Australia?

A warranty term cannot remove non-excludable consumer guarantees. Damage caused by misuse is a separate question from a defective product.

How long do I have to reject faulty goods in the UK?

The Consumer Rights Act 2015 generally provides a 30-day short-term right to reject, followed by repair or replacement remedies.

What documents should I keep?

Order confirmation, warranty text, the battery label photo and delivery records.
