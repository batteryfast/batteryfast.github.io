How to replace an iRobot Roomba battery

Roomba battery access is usually through the underside. The important step is confirming the pack family before you close the cover.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Before opening the robot

  • Turn the robot off and remove it from the dock.
  • Read the model sticker on the underside.
  • Confirm the part number on the original battery.
  • Check Combo status for j-series robots.
  • Work on a clean, dry surface.
Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

Fitting sequence

  1. Turn the robot over and open the battery compartment.
  2. Remove the old pack and note its orientation.
  3. Compare part number, voltage and plug shape with the new pack.
  4. Insert the new pack and secure the cover.
  5. Place the robot on the dock and charge fully.
  6. If the app reports an error, follow the reset guide.

See reset and errors after installation.

After the swap

Run a cleaning cycle and check that the robot completes it and returns to dock normally. Keep the old pack for recycling. If the robot refuses to charge, recheck the plug family before attempting more resets.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need tools to open a Roomba?

Many models use a coin slot or screw. Use the correct fastener and do not force the cover.

Should I reset the robot every time?

Not always. Charge fully first; reset only if status reporting or charging behaves abnormally.

Can I fit a Combo pack into a non-Combo robot?

Do not assume so. Confirm the plug family and part number first.
