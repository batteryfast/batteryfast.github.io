How to replace a Dyson vacuum battery

A correct pack should seat without force. This guide covers the general sequence across slide-in, screw-in and click-in Dyson families.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Preparation

  • Switch off and unplug the charger.
  • Work on a clean, dry surface away from metal clutter.
  • Keep the old pack until the new one is confirmed working.
  • Check the part number, voltage and connector against your compatibility chart.
Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

Removal and fitting

  1. Lay the vacuum on a soft surface.
  2. Open the battery bay: slide rail, screw canister or click catch.
  3. Lift out the old pack and compare labels side by side.
  4. Slide, screw or click the new pack into place without force.
  5. Verify the latch is fully seated and the housing is closed.
  6. Charge with the charger specified for the machine before first use.

Illustration: Dyson battery fitting steps.

If something feels wrong

Stop if the pack will not sit flat, the housing will not close or the machine reports a fault. Do not improvise a spacer or adapter. Go back to the compatibility chart or request a fit check.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to discharge the old battery first?

Not usually. Follow the new pack's instructions and use the correct charger.

Can I reuse the old screws?

Only if they are undamaged and intended for that housing. Do not overtighten plastic parts.

Is it normal for the new pack to feel loose?

No. A correctly specified pack should seat firmly without wobble.
