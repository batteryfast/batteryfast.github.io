Dyson V6 vs V7 vs V8 battery comparison

All three are older 21.6 V-class stick vacuums in many listings, but they are not a single battery. Fit comes first; value comparison comes second.

Which is the better value?

If you own one of these machines, the best value is the correct, honestly rated pack with a clear warranty. Capacity differences matter only among packs that fit your model. Compare watt-hours, not the headline mAh.

Fit warnings

Never substitute across these families because the voltage class matches. Shell geometry and connectors differ. Verify the chart before ordering.

Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

Frequently asked questions

Can I upgrade a V6 to a V8 battery?

Not as a like-for-like pack. They are different families. Use only a seller-confirmed cross-reference.

Which has longer runtime?

It depends on pack condition, capacity claim and cleaning mode. Compare watt-hours within the same family.

Are SV codes enough to choose?

No. They are a cross-check alongside part number, voltage and connector.
