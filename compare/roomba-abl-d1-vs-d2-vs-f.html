Roomba ABL-D1 vs ABL-D2 vs ABL-F comparison

These three codes cover much of the modern i, e and j range. The differences are small on a specification sheet and decisive in the battery bay.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

The Combo boundary

Combo j-series models are commonly listed with D2-style packs. ABL-D1 is generally associated with vacuum-only models. Read Combo versus non-Combo for the full decision path.

Suffix mattersListings mention ABL-D2A and ABL-D2B. Match the full part number, not just the prefix.

Capacity claims and app reporting

Higher capacity is useful only in the correct plug family with a truthful rating. After fitting, charge fully and check the app. See reset and errors.

Frequently asked questions

Is ABL-F better than ABL-D1?

It is higher capacity in many listings, but it is not automatically compatible. Fit decides.

Are D1 and D2 plugs identical?

They are often described as similar-looking. Do not interchange them.

Which pack does j7+ use?

Listings commonly point to D2-style or ABL-F families. Check the original pack part number.
