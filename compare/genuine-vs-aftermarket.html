Genuine versus aftermarket vacuum batteries

A replacement pack is a safety component. Whether it is branded or aftermarket, the deciding factors are traceable specifications, correct fit and enforceable warranty terms.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Consumer law by country

Australia: ACCC guidance confirms that consumer guarantees are automatic and cannot be removed by a warranty. United Kingdom: the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives remedies against the retailer. United States: rely on the written seller warranty and state law.

Australia - ACL and ACCCConsumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law apply automatically and cannot be excluded by a store warranty. A seller's written warranty is additional to those rights. Ask for warranty and return terms in writing before checkout.
United KingdomThe Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you remedies against the retailer for goods that are not of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose or as described. Check delivery, returns and recycling terms before you buy.
United StatesConfirm the exact part number and the seller's return window. For disposal, use a certified battery drop-off or household hazardous waste program; do not put lithium-ion packs in household bins.

Making the call

  1. Confirm fit and part number first.
  2. Ask for cell and protection details for aftermarket packs.
  3. Read warranty and return text in full.
  4. Compare delivered price including shipping.
  5. Keep documentation and recycle the old pack.

Frequently asked questions

Does aftermarket always mean lower quality?

No, but quality varies more. Ask for specifications and warranty rather than judging by price.

Can a seller refuse a return because I fitted the battery?

That depends on the cause and local law. In Australia, consumer guarantees cannot be excluded by a store policy.

Which is cheaper overall?

The pack that fits correctly, lasts and has clear warranty terms. A cheap mismatched pack is rarely cheaper.
