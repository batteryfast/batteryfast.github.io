Frequently asked questions

Which comparison should I read first?

Start with your model family comparison, then move to genuine versus aftermarket and runtime versus price.

Do comparison pages replace a compatibility chart?

No. Use the chart to confirm fit, then use comparisons to choose among compatible options.

Are the capacity figures universal?

No. Sellers publish different figures. Confirm the label and compare watt-hours.
