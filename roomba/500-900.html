Roomba 500 to 900 series battery guide

The classic numeric Roombas span many models and two battery chemistries. Matching the old pack is more reliable than matching a series number alone.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Runtime expectations for older robots

An older robot may return to dock early because the battery has aged, but dirty sensors, brushes and a full bin can produce the same symptom. Clean the robot and run a full charge cycle before judging a new pack.

Frequently asked questions

Can I upgrade a 600-series Roomba to lithium-ion?

Some sellers list compatible upgrades, but confirm with the exact model and charger before changing chemistry.

Why does my Roomba 980 still stop early?

Battery age is common, but sensor dirt and heavy debris also cause early docking. Check the basics first.

Do I need to discharge a NiMH pack fully?

Follow the manufacturer's guidance for that pack. Modern advice varies by chemistry and design.
