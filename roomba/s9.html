Roomba s9 and s9+ battery guide

The s9 flagship uses a distinct battery family. It is not interchangeable with the i, e or j series packs, even where capacity figures look similar.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Why s9 packs are separate

The s9 body, dock and battery management are specific to that flagship line. A pack made for an i7 or j7 is not a drop-in substitute. Do not use an adapter or modify the connector.

Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

Buying and warranty checks

Ask for the exact part number, warranty length and return terms. In Australia, statutory consumer guarantees apply alongside any seller warranty; in the UK, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 applies; in the US, check the written policy. See warranty and returns.

Frequently asked questions

Can an ABL-D2 pack fit an s9?

No. ABL-D2 is commonly listed for i/e/j families, not the s9 flagship. Confirm the ABL-B part number.

How long does an s9 battery last?

Life depends on cleaning frequency and conditions. Many sellers discuss a multi-year range, but your pack's condition is the real indicator.

After fitting, why does the app still show low battery?

Charge fully and follow the reset guidance. If the fault persists, recheck the part number and connector.
