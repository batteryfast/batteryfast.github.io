Roomba i7, i8 and j7 battery guide

These robots are often grouped together, yet their packs are not one item. The plug type and Combo status decide the answer.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Do not rely on the robot name aloneAsk the seller to confirm the exact part number and plug type against your robot's model sticker.

Why the Combo question changes everything

Combo robots add mopping hardware and are commonly listed with a different plug family. A vacuum-only pack may not communicate or charge correctly in a Combo model. Read Combo versus non-Combo before choosing.

Capacity and app behaviour

Sellers publish several capacity figures for ABL packs. Confirm watt-hours and ask how the figure was measured. After installation, a full charge and a reset may be needed for the app to report accurately. See reset and errors.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put ABL-F in an i7?

Some listings cross-reference it, but others do not. Match the part number on your original battery and get written confirmation.

Is a higher capacity pack always better?

Only if it is compatible and honestly rated. Compare watt-hours, warranty and fit evidence.

Do I need to reset my i7 after a battery swap?

Often yes. Fit the pack, charge fully, then follow the model-specific reset steps in our reset guide.
