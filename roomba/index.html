Frequently asked questions

How do I know which ABL battery my Roomba uses?

Flip the robot and read the model sticker, then compare the part number printed on the original battery. Do not rely on colour or shape alone.

Are ABL-D1 and ABL-D2 interchangeable?

They may look similar but are commonly listed for different plug and model sets. Confirm the part number and Combo status.

Does Batteryfast sell batteries?

No. We publish compatibility guidance and link to specialist retailers for current stock and terms.
