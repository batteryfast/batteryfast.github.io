Roomba reset and error messages after a battery swap

A new pack should be detected, but the robot and app may need a reset before they report normal status. Work through the simple checks before assuming the battery is faulty.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

First checks after fitting

  1. Confirm the part number matches the old pack or a written fit list from the seller.
  2. Seat the pack squarely; do not force the connector.
  3. Charge fully using the correct dock and adapter.
  4. Check that the contacts on the robot and pack are clean and dry.
  5. Restart the app and re-read battery status.
If it clicks but will not chargeA mechanical fit is not an electrical match. Recheck the part number and plug family before trying repeated resets.

When to stop resetting

Repeated resets will not fix a wrong pack, a damaged connector or a failing dock. If the same error returns after a full charge and a reseat, ask the seller to confirm the part number in writing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reset a Roomba after a battery change?

Charge fully, reseat the pack and follow your model's reset steps in the official manual. Avoid opening the battery.

Why does my Roomba report a wrong battery level?

It can be a charge-cycle issue, a communication mismatch or a wrong pack family. Confirm the part number first.

Do I need to replace the dock too?

Not usually. Inspect the contacts and adapter first; a faulty dock can mimic a battery problem.
