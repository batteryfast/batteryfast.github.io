Dyson V12 Detect Slim battery guide

The V12 uses a slimmer pack format. That single design choice rules out most older Dyson batteries, regardless of what a generic compatibility list claims.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

V12 pack identity

SV codes reported for V12 Detect Slim variants include SV20, SV26, SV34, SV44 and SV46. These identify machine variants; they are not battery part numbers. Read the pack label for voltage, capacity and part number before comparing any listing.

Machine family
V12 Detect Slim and related slim variants
SV examples
SV20, SV26, SV34, SV44, SV46 (verify on plate)
Fit rule
Slim shell and specific connector; no assumption of cross-fit

Why a V8 or V10 pack is not a substitute

The V12 shell, mounting points and centre of gravity are different. Even where a pack physically clicks in, the battery management handshake may fail or the machine may limit power. Never modify a connector or spacer to make a pack fit.

Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

What to send for a fit check

Send the SV code, the part number from the old battery, a clear photo of the label and the battery bay. A fit check should end with a written confirmation, not an estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a universal Dyson V12 battery?

No. Different V12 variants are listed against different codes. Use the part number on your original pack.

Can a higher capacity pack improve V12 runtime?

Only if it is genuinely compatible and honestly rated. Compare watt-hours and verify fit before price.

What if my V12 battery is swollen?

Stop using it, isolate it from heat and flammable material, and use an approved recycling route. Do not open it.
