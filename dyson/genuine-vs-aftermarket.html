Genuine versus aftermarket Dyson batteries

The choice is not simply brand versus price. It is about traceable specifications, safe construction and clear warranty terms for your country.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

What 'genuine' should mean

A genuine pack is sold against the original manufacturer part number and supported by the manufacturer terms. An aftermarket pack can still be a sound choice when the seller states voltage, capacity, cell type, protection circuitry and a written warranty.

Warranty and consumer rights

In Australia, voluntary warranties sit alongside non-excludable consumer guarantees under the ACL. In the UK, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives remedies against the retailer. In the US, check the written warranty from the seller and return policy. Fault caused by misuse or incorrect installation is a separate matter from a defective product.

Australia - ACL and ACCCConsumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law apply automatically and cannot be excluded by a store warranty. A seller's written warranty is additional to those rights. Ask for warranty and return terms in writing before checkout.
United KingdomThe Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you remedies against the retailer for goods that are not of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose or as described. Check delivery, returns and recycling terms before you buy.
United StatesConfirm the exact part number and the seller's return window. For disposal, use a certified battery drop-off or household hazardous waste program; do not put lithium-ion packs in household bins.

A practical checklist

  1. Confirm the part number and voltage against your old pack.
  2. Ask what cells and protection circuitry are used.
  3. Read the warranty and returns text, not just the headline.
  4. Check that the seller will confirm fit in writing.
  5. Keep the old pack for recycling, not for resale.

Frequently asked questions

Does an aftermarket battery void my Dyson warranty?

It depends on the claim and the cause. In Australia, consumer guarantees cannot be excluded by a warranty term. Ask the manufacturer and seller for their written position.

Are aftermarket capacities trustworthy?

Some are, some are not. Compare watt-hours, ask for cell information and read independent reviews.

Should I pay more for an OEM pack?

Pay more when you value manufacturer documentation and terms. Pay less only when the seller provides clear specifications and warranty.
