Dyson V10 battery guide

V10 listings are a common source of confusion because sellers describe the same platform in different ways. The label on your pack resolves it.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

What voltage does a Dyson V10 battery have?

Market listings variously describe V10 packs as 25.2 V, or as 21.6 V nominal and 25.2 V when charged. Rather than memorising a number, read the voltage line printed on your original battery. If a listing disagrees with your label, do not order.

V10 sub-models and fit

Absolute, Animal, Motorhead and Total Clean machines are often grouped together, but sellers do not all describe the same shell. Confirm the battery bay shape before buying. Do not assume a V8 pack will fit a V10 because both are stick vacuums.

Fit checkRemove the pack, photograph the label and the bay, and compare part numbers. A V10 pack is physically larger than many older Dyson packs.

Capacity claims and price

Compare watt-hours and ask how the capacity was measured. A suspiciously high mAh figure at a very low price deserves scrutiny. See runtime versus price and genuine versus aftermarket.

Frequently asked questions

Is SV12 a battery part number?

No. SV12 is a machine code commonly associated with V10. The battery part number is printed on the pack itself.

Can I fit a Dyson V10 battery myself?

Usually yes with basic care, provided the correct pack is used. Follow the steps in our installation guide and never force a connector.

Why do sellers quote different voltages?

Nominal versus charged voltage is often the reason. Use the printed label as your deciding reference.
