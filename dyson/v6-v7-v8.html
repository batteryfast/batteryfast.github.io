Dyson V6, V7 and V8 battery guide

These three generations sit in the same broad voltage family, but they are not one universal pack. The differences are in the shell, connector and part number.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

A higher capacity claim is only useful if the pack is genuinely compatible and honestly rated. Compare watt-hours, not mAh alone.

Can I use one pack across V6, V7 and V8?

Treat them as separate families. A V6 slide-in pack and a V7 canister pack may share a voltage class but use different mounting and contacts. A pack that must be forced into place is the wrong pack.

Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

Runtime, age and realistic expectations

Runtime falls as cells age and when the floor head, filter or bin is restricted. Before buying a new pack, clean the machine and check whether the drop happens in boost mode only. For the price logic, see runtime versus price.

Frequently asked questions

Is a V7 battery the same as a V8 battery?

No. They are in the same voltage family but are sold against different part numbers and fit lists. Check the label.

Why does my V8 still die quickly after charging?

A restricted filter or motorised head can shorten runtime, as can an ageing pack. Clean the machine first, then test with the original charger.

What does SV03 mean?

It is a Dyson machine variant code often associated with V6-era handhelds. It is a cross-check, not a substitute for the battery part number.
