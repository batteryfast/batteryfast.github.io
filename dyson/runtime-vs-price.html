Dyson battery runtime versus price

A bigger mAh number on a box does not automatically mean longer cleaning. Energy is measured in watt-hours, and usable runtime also depends on the machine and floor head.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

The calculation that matters

Formula
Wh = V x Ah
Example A
21.6 V x 4.0 Ah = 86.4 Wh
Example B
25.2 V x 4.0 Ah = 100.8 Wh
Why it matters
The same mAh at a higher voltage stores more energy

Use watt-hours to compare claims across platforms. Then divide price by watt-hours only among packs that are genuinely compatible with your machine.

Why advertised runtime rarely matches real cleaning

  • Manufacturer figures are often measured in a low-power mode without a motorised floor head.
  • Boost mode, deep carpet, pet hair and a blocked filter all reduce runtime.
  • Ageing cells hold less charge, so an old pack can fall well short of its original figure.
  • A higher-capacity pack cannot fix a mechanical restriction or a failing motor.
Honest comparisonAsk whether a seller states both voltage and capacity. A lone mAh figure is not enough to compare value.

Price, warranty and total cost

A slightly higher price with a clear warranty, certified cells and a written fit confirmation can be cheaper than a low-cost pack that fails early. Read genuine versus aftermarket and runtime versus price compared.

Frequently asked questions

Is 4000 mAh always better than 3000 mAh?

Only within the same voltage platform and if the capacity claim is honest. Compare watt-hours and part numbers.

How do I estimate cost per watt-hour?

Divide the delivered price by the stated Wh, then compare only packs that fit your machine and include similar warranty terms.

Will a new battery restore original runtime?

It should improve runtime if the cells were the cause, but filters, floor heads and cleaning mode also matter.
