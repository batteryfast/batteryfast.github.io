How to fit a Dyson replacement battery yourself

Most Dyson battery swaps are straightforward when you have the correct pack. The job is not to make a battery fit; it is to confirm that it already does.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Before you start

  • Switch the vacuum off and unplug the charger.
  • Work on a clean, dry, non-metallic surface.
  • Have the old battery, the new battery and the original charger to hand.
  • Read both labels and confirm voltage, capacity and part number agree.
  • Do not use force, metal tools against contacts or any improvised adapter.
Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

Fitting sequence

  1. Place the vacuum upside down on a soft surface.
  2. Locate the battery release: a slide-in rail, screw canister or click-in catch depending on model.
  3. Remove the old pack gently and set it aside for label comparison.
  4. Compare the part number, voltage line and connector with the new pack.
  5. Seat the new pack until it locks naturally. If it needs force, stop.
  6. Charge with the correct charger before first use and check that the machine powers normally.

See the illustrated installation guide.

After the swap

Record the date and keep the old pack for recycling. If the machine reports a fault, remove and reseat the pack once, then follow the model-specific support route. Do not open the pack to "reset" it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need special tools?

Usually only a suitable screwdriver for screw-in packs. If a pack needs force or improvised tools, the fit is wrong.

Should I charge a new Dyson battery before first use?

Follow the new pack's instructions and use the correct charger. A full initial charge helps confirm the pack and charger communicate correctly.

What do I do with the old battery?

Protect the terminals and use an approved battery recycling or household hazardous waste route. See our regional recycling guide.
