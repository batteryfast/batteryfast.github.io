Dyson V11 and V15 battery compatibility

For later full-size Dyson stick vacuums, the honest answer is usually: check the pack label before anyone quotes a fit. V11 and V15 are not single-battery families.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Click-in versus screw-in V11

Early V11 machines are commonly described as screw-in, while later versions use a click-in release. They are not the same pack. The SV code on the body plate and the part number on the battery must agree with the listing.

Why older Dyson packs are not a shortcut

Voltage, connector pinout, battery management communication and shell geometry all differ. An adapter or a pack that only partly seats is a safety problem, not a bargain. We do not publish unverified V11, V12 or V15 specifications.

Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

A careful buying sequence

  1. Read the SV code from the machine plate.
  2. Remove the battery and photograph every line on the label.
  3. Compare part number, voltage and capacity with a retailer listing.
  4. Ask the seller to confirm fit in writing before checkout.
  5. Keep proof of purchase and warranty terms.

Frequently asked questions

Do V11 and V15 share a battery?

Some click-in variants are described as sharing a platform, but the reliable check is the part number printed on your own pack.

Can I use a V10 battery in a V11?

Do not assume so. Different shell, connector and management requirements make this an unsafe substitution.

What if a seller cannot confirm the part number?

Treat that as a stop sign. Ask for the part number in writing or use a specialist fit-check service.
