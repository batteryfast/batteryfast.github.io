Dyson not holding charge - what to check first

A short runtime is not always a dead battery. Work through the cheap checks first so you do not replace a pack when the real cause is elsewhere.

A note on wording: Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guidance publisher, not the original equipment manufacturer. Brand and model names below identify the machine platform only.

Reviewed 2026-11-25 by the Batteryfast compatibility desk · Specifications must be confirmed on your own battery label.

Check the label before you orderTreat the voltage, capacity (Ah/Wh), part number and connector printed on your original pack as the source of truth. A similar-looking shell is not proof of fit.

Quick diagnosis sequence

  1. Clean the filter and empty the bin; a restriction makes the motor work harder.
  2. Check the wand, brush bar and inlet for blockages.
  3. Try the original charger on the same outlet and confirm the charge light behaves normally.
  4. Note whether the drop happens only in Boost mode.
  5. Record the battery label, age and any fault indicator before contacting a seller.

Illustration: fault diagnosis flow.

Signs the pack itself is at end of life

  • Runtime has fallen steadily over months of normal use.
  • The pack gets unusually hot during charging.
  • The machine cuts out even on a full charge in low-power mode.
  • The battery is swollen, cracked or leaking - stop using it immediately.
Safety firstNever open, splice, short or adapt a lithium-ion pack. Stop using a swollen, hot, leaking or damaged battery and recycle it through an approved program.

When to replace rather than repair

If the machine is otherwise in good condition and the pack is the limiting factor, a correct replacement is usually more economical than a new vacuum. Start with the compatibility chart, then read runtime versus price.

Keep your recordsPhotograph the old label before disposal. Warranty and return discussions are much easier with the part number and purchase date to hand.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my Dyson last two minutes in Boost?

Boost draws far more current. Short Boost runtime can be normal even with a healthy pack; compare low-power runtime first.

Can a blocked filter look like a battery fault?

Yes. Restricted airflow increases load and shortens runtime. Clean the machine before concluding the battery has failed.

Is a hot battery normal?

Mild warmth while charging can be normal. A hot, swollen or smelling pack is not - stop using it and recycle it safely.
