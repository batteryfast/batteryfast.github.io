Built for Australia, the UK and the US

Buying rules are not the same everywhere. Our regional guide explains Australian Consumer Law and ACCC consumer guarantees, UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 remedies, and US purchase, shipping and battery recycling considerations. We do not quote prices we cannot verify and we do not publish unverified specifications.

Australia - ACL and ACCCConsumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law apply automatically and cannot be excluded by a store warranty. A seller's written warranty is additional to those rights. Ask for warranty and return terms in writing before checkout.
United KingdomThe Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives you remedies against the retailer for goods that are not of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose or as described. Check delivery, returns and recycling terms before you buy.
United StatesConfirm the exact part number and the seller's return window. For disposal, use a certified battery drop-off or household hazardous waste program; do not put lithium-ion packs in household bins.

Frequently asked questions

How do I identify my Dyson battery?

Read the SV code on the machine plate and the voltage, capacity and part number printed on the original battery. Match all four before ordering.

Can a Roomba Combo use a non-Combo battery?

Not safely as a rule. Combo models commonly use a different connector and pack family. Check the part number on your old battery first.

Do you sell batteries?

No. Batteryfast is an independent compatibility guide. We link to specialist retailers where you can check current stock and terms.
